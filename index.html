About Google Translate

Google Translate is a multilingual translation service developed by Google that allows users to translate text, speech, images, and websites between hundreds of languages. It is widely used around the world for quick and accessible communication across language barriers.

The service uses advanced machine learning technology, specifically neural machine translation (NMT), to provide more accurate and natural-sounding translations. Instead of translating word-by-word, it analyzes entire sentences to better understand context and meaning.

Key Features

  • Text translation between over 100 languages
  • Voice input and real-time speech translation
  • Camera translation for signs, menus, and documents
  • Offline translation support
  • Website translation directly in your browser

Why It Matters

Google Translate helps people communicate globally, making information more accessible regardless of language. Whether you're traveling, learning a new language, or just curious, it breaks down communication barriers and connects people worldwide.

This Whale Talk project is inspired by the idea of translation; taking something complex (like whale sounds or encoded language) and converting it into something humans can understand.
